Important safety note

This system can shortlist donors with the same recorded blood group. It cannot confirm blood compatibility, donor fitness, blood availability or suitability for transfusion.

All donor selection, screening, blood grouping confirmation and crossmatching must be performed by authorized health professionals. Do not use this prototype to make clinical decisions.
